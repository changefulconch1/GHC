THE PROVING GROUND

ASIA'S 422M VACUUM CIRCUIT

Located at the Discovery Campus of IIT Madras in Thaiyur. Spanning a 2-meter diameter continuous steel tube under sub-millibar atmospheric conditions.

SECTOR 01 // 0m - 20m

AIR-LOCK LAUNCH BAY

High-pressure hermetic sealing and pre-launch isolation gate.

SECTOR 02 // 20m - 150m

LIM CATAPULT ACCELERATION

Dual-sided linear induction stator rails delivering 2.4G thrust.

SECTOR 03 // 150m - 320m

SUPERSONIC LEVITATION

Mach 1.0 cruise zone with 15mm stable magnetic airgap.

SECTOR 04 // 320m - 400m

EDDY REGENERATIVE BRAKE

Frictionless copper-plate magnetic deceleration under 2.5G.

SECTOR 05 // 400m - 422m

TERMINAL ARRESTOR DOCK

Hydraulic fail-safe arrestors and egress repressurization.

6mm

PATENTED THIN-WALL STEEL VACUUM TUBE

Engineered by Avishkar Hyperloop & IIT Madras. While global test tracks require bulky 16–18mm steel walls to withstand vacuum pressure, GHC achieved an ultra-efficient 6mm reinforced wall design, slashing manufacturing costs by 65% and setting a worldwide benchmark for commercial scalability.
